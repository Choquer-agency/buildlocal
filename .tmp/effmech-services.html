Specialty Services

Air Advice

Ever wonder just what you are exposed to that causes your family to suffer from illness, respiratory infections, asthma and allergies?

Efficiency Mechanical can install a recording device that measures the level of carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, chemical pollutants and particulates in your home. The resulting data will help us make recommendations based on real measurements in your home. We can help you decide what sort of additional products would be beneficial to your family’s home environment including air cleaners, humidifiers and duct cleaning.

Duct Cleaning

At Efficiency Mechanical, we are strictly in the HVAC business. We don’t offer carpet cleaning, plumbing and tile cleaning like other companies in the valley. We specialize in whole-house comfort whether your home is from the ground up or an existing home, we have the “whole package” when it comes to taking care of your equipment and your indoor air quality.

Our focus on HVAC work means that when we say we are cleaning your ducts….we truly are. We are not only cleaning them with our truck-mounted system, but sanitizing them as well. Why trust the carpet cleaning company to work on a critical component of your HVAC system?

Duct cleaning should be done in your home every 2-5 years depending on how you use your home. If there are heavy smokers and four-legged family members in the home, the ductwork should be cleaned more often.

We also have cameras that can see inside your ductwork whether it is above ground or under ground. We can see tears in the ductwork as well as obstructions such as bird’s nests. If you have ductwork that is difficult to access, a camera is the ideal tool to use.
