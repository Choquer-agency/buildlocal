Common Questions About Our Services

We believe in clear communication and complete transparency about our services. Here are detailed answers to questions we hear most often from Phoenix area homeowners about our comprehensive HVAC and plumbing solutions for their homes.

How quickly can you respond to AC emergencies?

King Charles LLC treats cooling issues as a priority during Phoenix summers and schedules urgent repairs as soon as possible within our business hours. We understand how unbearable Phoenix heat can be and work hard to restore your comfort the same day whenever we can.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes, we provide comprehensive membership programs that include regular tune-ups, priority service scheduling, and other exclusive benefits. These plans help prevent unexpected breakdowns, extend equipment life, and give you peace of mind knowing your system is properly maintained year-round.

What areas do you service?

We proudly serve 20 locations across the entire Phoenix metropolitan area, from Anthem in the north to Chandler in the south. Our technicians know these communities well, understand local conditions, and arrive prepared with common parts to complete most repairs in one visit.

Are your technicians certified?

All our technicians hold proper ROC licensing for both commercial and residential HVAC and plumbing work. This unique dual training means we can handle complete home comfort solutions, saving you from calling multiple contractors when issues involve both systems.

Do you handle permit requirements?

King Charles LLC manages all necessary permits for installations and major repairs, taking this burden completely off your shoulders. We handle the paperwork, inspections, and compliance requirements so you can focus on enjoying your newly comfortable home without the bureaucratic hassles.
