Things To Do In Peoria, AZ

1. Visit Lake Pleasant Regional Park

Lake Pleasant Regional Park is a beautiful outdoor destination located just north of Peoria. It features more than 10,000 acres of desert surrounded by mountains, which makes it a perfect spot for hiking, fishing, boating, and other water sports. The park has several picnic areas, campgrounds, and even a marina. You can rent boats and jet skis, or take a guided tour around the lake. It’s an ideal place to spend a day or a weekend.

2. Go Shopping at Arrowhead Towne Center

If you’re looking to do some shopping, Arrowhead Towne Center is the place to go in Peoria. It’s a mall that offers over 180 stores and restaurants, including popular brands like Macy’s, Dillard’s, JCPenney, and Apple. The mall also features a movie theater and plenty of entertainment options for kids. The mall is also home to several events throughout the year, including concerts, craft fairs, and holiday celebrations.

3. Golf at Quintero Golf Club

Quintero Golf Club is one of the best golf courses in Arizona, located about 20 minutes north of Peoria. It features breathtaking views, challenging terrain, and exceptional golfing experiences. The course is surrounded by the Sonoran Desert and offers several tee boxes, greens, and bunkers that challenge players of all levels. The clubhouse at Quintero offers an excellent restaurant and bar that is open to the public, even if you’re not a golfer.

4. Explore the Desert Botanical Garden

The Desert Botanical Garden is a unique, outdoor experience that showcases the beauty and diversity of desert plants and ecosystems. Located in Phoenix, just a short drive from Peoria, the garden features thousands of plants from around the world and hosts many events and exhibitions throughout the year. The garden is an ideal place for nature lovers, horticulture enthusiasts, and anyone interested in the desert ecosystem.

5. Visit the Challenger Space Center

The Challenger Space Center in Peoria is an exciting and educational destination that is perfect for families and science enthusiasts of all ages. This nonprofit organization offers a wide variety of space-related exhibits, educational programs, and guided tours. Interactive displays, simulators, and hands-on activities offer visitors a chance to learn about space exploration, astronomy, and the science and technology behind it all.

Conclusion

Peoria, Arizona, is a beautiful, diverse, and exciting place to visit. Whether you’re looking for outdoor adventures, shopping, golfing, or cultural experiences, Peoria has plenty to offer. From Lake Pleasant Regional Park to Arrowhead Towne Center, Quintero Golf Club, Desert Botanical Garden, and Challenger Space Center, there is something for everyone in Peoria.
