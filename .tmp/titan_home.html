Tile

Tile roofing is a popular option in Arizona for a reason. Celebrated for its energy efficiency, durability, and aesthetic appeal, it stands the test of time with style. At Titan, we specialize in tile roofing installation, repair, and maintenance services and work with a variety of tile materials. Let our pros help you select a winning option for your tastes and budget.

Shingle

Shingle roofs are a classic, timeless choice for homeowners and businesses alike. At Titan, we offer a wide array range of shingle materials and colors to satisfy every style and price range. With years of experience in shingle roof installation, repair, and maintenance, our team is here to ensure that your roof will provide durable protection for years to come.

Foam

Want an energy-efficient, durable option for your roof? Look no further than foam. Made from high-density polyurethane, these roofs provide excellent insulation and can help reduce energy bills by up to 30%. Foam roofs are also easy to install and maintain, making them a cost-effective option for many property owners. Get in touch to dive into the details!
