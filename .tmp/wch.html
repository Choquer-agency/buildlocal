Frequently Asked Questions

What Is the Best Thermostat Setting to Use for My Heating System?

Choosing the right thermostat setting for your heating system largely depends on how cold it is. The most common setting in the winter seems to be 70°-72°. The trick is to avoid drastic temperature changes. For instance, don’t set your temperature back more than 5° as the unit will be forced to work harder to achieve the desired temperature.

How Often Should I Replace My AC Filters?

The frequency of replacing your air filters could depend on factors such as the air pollution levels around your house, household size, number of pets, complete indoor air quality and air filter model, among other things. Manufacturers typically recommend replacing basic 1”- 3” filters every one to two months.

What HVAC Products/Brands Do You Provide?

Wolfgangs Cooling, Heating & Plumbing provides top of the line products from the industry’s most trusted brands. We offer furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, in-duct air purifiers, and more. Call or text us to help you decide the best products for your home.

When Should I Call A Plumber?

When you notice that something isn't right with your plumbing system, you should call your local plumber. Some issues include: water leaking through the ceiling, no hot water, clogged drains, smelly garbage disposals, a faucet that won't turn on, or water in the basement.

What Plumbing Services Do You Offer?

At Wolfgangs, we offer a variety of different residential and commercial plumbing services, including: drain and sewer line cleaning, leak detection, whole house water filtration, icemaker lines, gas connections, drinking water purification systems, and repair and replacements of sinks, toilets, water lines, water heaters, and garbage disposals.
