Frequently Asked Question

What is the average life span of an HVAC unit in AZ?

The average life span of an HVAC unit in Arizona is 12 to 15 years. This is lower than the national average of 15 to 20 years because of the higher temperatures and frequent usage of AC in Arizona. However, some other factors influence the longevity of an HVAC unit.

Why is new HVAC so expensive?

These are some main reasons a new HVAC system can be expensive, such as the type, size, efficiency, brand, and unit installation. However, a new HVAC system can also be a worthwhile investment in comfort, energy efficiency, and home value, and Your Glendale HVAC is the best option to get it in Glendale, AZ area.

Can an HVAC unit last 30 years?

It is very unlikely that an HVAC unit can last 30 years, as the average life expectancy of an HVAC system is between 10 and 20 years, depending on the type, quality, usage, and maintenance of the system. However, there may be some rare cases where an HVAC unit can last longer than 25 years if it is well-built, properly sized, installed by a professional, and regularly serviced by a qualified technician.

What time of year should you replace HVAC?

The best time of year to replace your HVAC system is during the off-season, which is fall or spring. During these seasons, the demand for HVAC services is lower, so you can get better deals and discounts from manufacturers and contractors.
