About Gen2Roofing of Phoenix, AZ

Gen2Roofing LLC is a licensed and bonded second-generation family operated business.

Our mission is to provide 100% quality, satisfaction, and reliable roofing services every time! With over 20 years of experience, we offer inspections, maintenance, repairs, or complete new roof installations.

We work with tile, shingles, and flat/patio roofs. No roof, and no project is too big or too small.

Our Philosophy For Success

The single and most important element in Gen2Roofing’s success is the satisfied customer. The customer’s trust and satisfaction with the quality of service provided is our #1 priority at all times.

Our Services

Residential Repairs & Installs

Gen2 Roofing can repair or install all types of residential roofs including tile roofs, shingle roofs, flat/foam roofs and patio roofs.  We service the entire Phoenix valley area

Commercial Repairs & Installs

Gen2 Roofing also has the expertise to service commercial roofs including flat and foam roofs.  We are experienced with expansion cracks, ponding water, roof jacks, blisters and elastomeric coastings.

Additional Services

In addition to roofing services, we are also experienced in other types of home projects including plumbing, electrical and interior and exterior remodel work.
