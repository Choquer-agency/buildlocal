We Specialize In

When the Arizona sun is blazing, our cooling services keep your home comfortable, efficient, and worry-free. Comfort Experts specializes in residential air conditioning in Mesa and the surrounding Valley, offering honest recommendations, no-pressure options, and fast response times when your AC decides to quit on the hottest day of the year.

Cooling specialties include:

  • AC repair in Mesa, AZ – Quick, accurate troubleshooting to get your system cooling again fast.

  • AC installation & replacement – High-efficiency air conditioners properly sized and installed for your home and budget.

  • Seasonal AC tune-ups & maintenance – Prevent surprise breakdowns, improve comfort, and help lower your energy bills.

  • Ductless mini-split cooling – Perfect for bonus rooms, garages, casitas, and hard-to-cool areas.

  • Thermostat upgrades & smart controls – Better comfort and control with modern, Wi-Fi–enabled thermostats.

  • Zoned cooling solutions – Customize temperatures in different parts of your home for maximum comfort.

When desert nights turn chilly, our heating services keep your home safe, warm, and comfortable all winter long. Comfort Experts specializes in residential heating in Mesa and the surrounding Valley, with honest diagnostics, clear options, and fast response times—without the scare tactics or surprise add-ons.

Heating specialties include:

  • Furnace repair & troubleshooting – Accurate diagnostics and reliable repairs to get your heat back on quickly.

  • New furnace installation & replacement – High-efficiency systems properly sized and installed for your home and budget.

  • Heat pump service & installation – Energy-efficient heating and cooling in one versatile system.

  • Seasonal heating tune-ups & safety checks – Help prevent breakdowns, improve performance, and catch safety issues early.

  • Gas & electric furnace maintenance – Keep your system running safely and efficiently all season long.

  • Thermostat upgrades & smart heating controls – Fine-tune your comfort and save energy with modern, Wi-Fi–enabled thermostats.

Between desert dust, pollen, and everyday indoor pollutants, the air inside your home can actually be more polluted than the air outside. Our indoor air quality services help Mesa homeowners breathe easier with cleaner, fresher, healthier air in every room—especially important for kids, allergy sufferers, and anyone with respiratory concerns.

Indoor Air Quality specialties include:

  • Whole-home air filtration & purification – High-efficiency filters and air cleaners that capture dust, pollen, dander, and more.

  • Duct cleaning & duct sealing – Remove built-up debris and help keep conditioned air (and your money) from leaking out.

  • Air scrubbers & UV light systems – Advanced technology to reduce airborne bacteria, viruses, and odors.

  • Humidity control solutions – Help balance dry indoor air for greater comfort and fewer irritants.

  • Filter upgrades & maintenance – Better-quality filters and reminders so you always stay on top of replacements.

  • Indoor air quality inspections – Honest assessments and tailored recommendations based on your home and family’s needs.

Keeping your building comfortable isn’t optional—it’s how you keep employees productive, customers happy, and equipment protected. Our commercial HVAC services support businesses across Mesa and the surrounding Valley with reliable cooling and heating solutions, tailored maintenance plans, and fast response times when every minute of downtime matters.

Commercial HVAC specialties include:

  • Commercial AC repair & service – Prompt, professional diagnostics and repairs to keep your business cool and operational.

  • Commercial heating repair & service – Reliable heating solutions for offices, restaurants, retail spaces, warehouses, and more.

  • New system design & installation – Properly sized rooftop units and split systems engineered for your building’s needs and usage.

  • Preventive maintenance agreements – Custom maintenance plans to reduce breakdowns, extend system life, and control operating costs.

  • Packaged & rooftop unit service – Expert care for RTUs and packaged systems common in commercial properties.

  • HVAC for tenant improvements & build-outs – Smart solutions for remodels, new suites, and changing space requirements.

  • Energy efficiency upgrades – Recommendations to help lower utility costs and improve comfort throughout your building.
