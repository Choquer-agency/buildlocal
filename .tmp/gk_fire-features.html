Custom Fire Features

Fire features bring warmth, ambiance, and year-round enjoyment to any Arizona outdoor space. With our cool evenings, mild winters, and long outdoor-living season, adding a fire element creates an inviting gathering place for family and friends. Grass Kings designs and builds custom fire features that complement your existing landscape, enhance your patio, and blend seamlessly with the natural desert surroundings.

We specialize in a wide range of fire feature styles, from modern linear fire pits to rustic stone fire bowls and built-in seating walls that form natural conversation areas. Whether you envision a simple gas fire pit or a fully integrated outdoor lounge space, we tailor each design to your lifestyle and home’s aesthetic. Our team uses durable, weather-resistant materials that withstand Arizona’s intense heat and seasonal monsoon conditions, ensuring your fire feature remains safe, functional, and beautiful over time.
