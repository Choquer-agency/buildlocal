                Frequently Asked Questions

                • 01.What warranties do you offer?

                  We offer manufacturer warranties on materials (up to 50 years) and our own workmanship warranty (typically 5–10 years depending on the job).

                • 02.How long will my new roof last?

                  It depends on the material. Asphalt shingles typically last 15–30 years, while metal and tile roofs can last 40–70 years or more with proper maintenance.

                • 03.Will my homeowner’s insurance cover the cost?

                  It depends on the damage and your policy. We can help you navigate the insurance claim process if your roof was damaged by a covered event like a storm.

                • 04.What if it rains during the job?

                  We monitor the weather closely and take measures to protect your home with tarps and underlayment if rain occurs during the project.

                • 05.Can you install a roof in winter or during rainy seasons?

                  Yes, we work year-round. Certain weather conditions may cause delays, but we take all precautions to protect your home during installation.

                • 06.Do I need to be home during installation?

                  Not necessarily. Many homeowners are not home during the project, but we’ll keep you updated throughout the process.

                • 07.How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

                  Signs like missing shingles, water stains on ceilings, or visible sagging may indicate you need a new roof. However, some issues can be resolved with repairs. We offer free inspections to assess your specific needs.

                • 08.How long does a typical roofing job take?

                  Most residential roofing jobs take 1–3 days, depending on the size of the roof and weather conditions.

                • 09.What types of roofing materials do you offer?

                  We install asphalt shingles, metal roofing, tile, and flat roofing systems. Our team can recommend the best option based on your budget, climate, and home style.

                • 10.How much will my new roof cost?

                  Roofing costs vary based on materials, roof size, and complexity. We provide free, no-obligation estimates after inspecting your roof.

                • 11.Do you offer financing or payment plans?

                  Yes, we work with trusted financing partners to offer affordable monthly payment options. Contact us to learn more.
