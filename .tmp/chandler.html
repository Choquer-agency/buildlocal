For years, our teams have worked side by side. Now, we’re officially one brand—which means you’ll continue to receive the same 5-star service you’ve come to know and trust, with even more options to keep your home running smoothly.

Here’s what this means for you:

Z

︎ Your service history and warranties remain fully protected.

Z

You’ll see licensed & trusted technicians at your door.

Z

We’ve expanded our services—adding Plumbing, Drains, Electrical, and Insulation services to our existing HVAC services.

From fixing a leak to installing a new outlet or upgrading your electrical panel, Penguin Air, Plumbing and Electrical has you covered.

Thank you for trusting us with your home. We look forward to continuing to serve you.

For now, you’ll continue to see Chandler Air vehicles and technicians when we arrive at your home. As we move through the transition, service will gradually shift to Penguin Air, Plumbing & Electrical vehicles over the next few months. No matter which logo you see, you can expect the same licensed professionals and the same 5-star service you trust.
